# Client agreement note: Bakkerij Verhaeghe meal voucher scheme

Client: Bakkerij Verhaeghe, PC 118. Recorded by Anke Peeters (Client Services BE), 4 November 2025, after the meeting with Mieke Verhaeghe.

## Decision

From 1 January 2026 the client moves to the **maximum meal voucher scheme**:

- Face value **EUR 8.00** per day worked
- Employer contribution **EUR 6.91**
- Employee contribution **EUR 1.09**

Reason: the PC 118 sector CLA of 2025 raised the mandatory sector minimum and the client decided to go to the legal maximum at the same time to stay competitive for bakers.

## Actions

- Individual agreement addendum signed by all 42 employees, filed in the client DMS.
- Payroll parameter update scheduled for the January 2026 run. Confirmed done on 9 January 2026.
- Old value (EUR 5.91 employer share) no longer applies.
