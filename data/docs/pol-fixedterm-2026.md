# Fixed-term contracts Belgium: early termination and notice

Owner: Payroll Legal BE. Applies to all Belgian clients.

## Rule

A fixed-term contract normally ends on its end date without notice. Early termination is only possible:

1. **During the first half of the contract, and at most during the first 6 months**: either party can terminate with the normal notice period for an open-ended contract (based on seniority; for less than 3 months seniority that is **1 week** for the employer).
2. **After that**: early termination without serious cause means paying compensation equal to the salary until the end date, capped at twice the notice period that would apply to an open-ended contract.

## Practical

- Notice must be given in writing, starting the Monday after it is sent.
- Successive fixed-term contracts are limited: maximum 4 contracts of at least 3 months each, total no longer than 2 years, unless approved by the labour inspectorate.
