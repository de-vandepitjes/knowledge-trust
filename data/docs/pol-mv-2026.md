# Meal vouchers Belgium: 2026 rules and maximum amounts

Owner: Payroll Legal BE. Applies to all Belgian clients unless a sector or company agreement provides otherwise.

## Maximum amounts (unchanged since 2016, confirmed for 2026)

- Maximum face value per voucher: **EUR 8.00**
- Maximum employer contribution: **EUR 6.91** per voucher
- Minimum employee contribution: **EUR 1.09** per voucher
- One voucher per day actually worked

## Conditions for exemption from social security contributions and tax

1. Granted under a CLA or individual written agreement.
2. Number of vouchers equals the number of days actually worked.
3. Voucher issued in the employee's name.
4. Validity 12 months, electronic only.
5. Employer contribution does not exceed EUR 6.91, employee contribution at least EUR 1.09.

If any condition is not met, the full voucher is treated as salary.

Sector agreements (joint committees) can set lower maximums or make vouchers mandatory. Always check the client's joint committee and any company-level agreement before applying the general maximum.
