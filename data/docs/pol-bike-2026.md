# Bicycle allowance Belgium 2026

Owner: Payroll Legal BE.

- Exempt allowance: EUR 0.36 per kilometre, capped at EUR 3,610 per year (2026 indexed amounts).
- Mandatory for all private-sector employees who commute by bike since 1 May 2023 (CLA 164), unless a sector CLA already provides one.
- Employer must ask the employee for a signed declaration of the route and days.
