# Meal vouchers Belgium rules (copy)

Copied from the Payroll Legal drive for the Ghent office. No owner assigned.

- Maximum face value per voucher: EUR 8.00
- Maximum employer contribution: EUR 6.91
- Minimum employee contribution: EUR 1.09
- One voucher per day worked, electronic, valid 12 months, granted by CLA or written agreement.

Note: check with Legal whether the 2025 indexation proposal was adopted.
