# SD Worx challenge — "Unlock the Knowledge Within"

> Find it. Understand it. Trust it.
> **How might we turn fragmented organisational knowledge into a trusted shared resource?**

Full brief: [participants-guide.pdf](./participants-guide.pdf), page 5.

## The problem in their words

- Knowledge lives in policies, manuals, procedures, checklists, emails, Teams, shared files, workflows, business apps and in people's heads.
- Finding is the easy part. A search returns ten answers; an AI assistant can summarise them. The hard question is **whether an answer is reliable, current and relevant to this customer, country or situation.**
- It is fundamentally a **trust** problem. A useful solution shows not only an answer but _why it deserves confidence_, where uncertainty remains, and who can help.

## Real stories they gave

1. Urgent customer question. AI finds three docs: one recently updated, one without an owner, one that may apply to another country. A colleague shares contradicting info from Teams. Employee has information but can't act with confidence.
2. Payroll consultant inherits a client portfolio. The handover was one document and a one-hour conversation years ago. Knowledge is spread across documents, chats, workflows, apps, business data and experts in different teams.

## What they ask for

- A **focused proof of concept**. Choose **one meaningful problem**. Don't solve everything.
- Focus on **one role, one workflow, one knowledge source or one trust signal**.
- Make the **moment of doubt tangible**, then show how the PoC moves someone from _"I found something"_ to _"I understand why I can rely on it."_
- Don't hide complexity behind a black box. Make trust **visible, explainable and useful**.
- Start from the moment of doubt/friction, not from a prescribed technology.
